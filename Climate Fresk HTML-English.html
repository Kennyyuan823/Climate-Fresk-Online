🌍 Climate Fresk Online — Instructions

Game purpose: To raise people’s awareness of Earth’s climate changes and emphasize sustainable development through causal‑effect chains. This is the online simulation of Climate Fresk. Place all cards onto canvas slots and draw causal arrows to reconstruct the full chain of climate change. The page resets to blank canvas automatically on opening.

📐 Layout

• Desktop: Left‑side Card library stores all cards; central area is the drawing canvas.

• Phone / iPad: Tap Open card library to bring cards up from bottom drawer.

• Dashed boxes on canvas: slots for placing cards.

• iPad supports pinch‑to‑zoom on canvas; single‑finger drag to pan. Zoom buttons are also at top‑right corner.

🎮 How to play

1. Drag cards from card library and drop them into dashed slots.

2. Tap a placed card to open context menu:

 ‑ Return to card library: send this card back to library.

 ‑ Connect to another card slot: start drawing arrow, then tap target card to complete link.

🔘 Button reference

• Rules: Open this help panel.

• Show standard answer: draw standard causal arrows only, your card positions stay unchanged. Editing is disabled while answer is shown.

• Undo standard answer: remove standard arrows and restore your own connections.

• Check my arrows: validate your drawn arrows. 🟢 Green = matches expected causal link; ⚫ Black = wrong or extra connection.

• Return all cards: move all cards back to library, slots remain empty.

• Reset whole canvas: fully reset everything to initial blank state.

• Open card library (mobile): pop‑up bottom drawer with cards.

• Zoom out / Zoom in / Reset view: adjust canvas scale; will NOT modify cards or connections.

💡 Tip

Refreshing browser clears all work. Data stays in your local browser only, nothing is uploaded to server.
